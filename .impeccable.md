# Design Context

Captured 2026-10-26 from the README, DESIGN.md section 1 and the owner's brief (Hazel reference: warm gradient bands, big editorial headings, product-mock hero, bento, giant footer wordmark; "apply our design system, but don't keep it boring"). Edit if wrong.

## Users
Startup founders, product and strategy teams, small agencies. They are deciding something (pitch, roadmap, positioning) and have been burned by confident AI answers they could not check. Usually on a laptop between meetings, sometimes on a phone. Many are in India and Asia.

## Job to be done
Get a competitor list they can defend: every claim sourced, every rejection visible, a human approving it.

## Brand personality
Calm, exact, plain-spoken. Confident because it shows its work, never because it shouts. Quiet like a well-run archive: the proof is the product's own output, not decoration.

## Aesthetic direction
Plain proof (revised 2026-10-28; the earlier "editorial ledger" with serif on cream, amber glow and footnote devices was rejected as naive and AI-looking). Near-monochrome white page, one grotesk (Instrument Sans, self-hosted) at weight 500 for display with tight tracking, a near-black CTA, whitespace instead of borders, real photographs (archives, annotated notes) with real product fragments floating on them, and the pipeline shown as a numbered flow of real UI fragments. References: near.com (minimalism), pallyy.com (real images), rulebase.co (type and spacing), workos.com/atlas (space over borders), orshot.com (flow visuals and icons). Rules: DESIGN.md section 15.

## Constraints
- Our design system wins: colors only from Ant tokens, spacing only from spacing.css, no inline styles, light mode only, WCAG AA, mobile-first. Fonts and photos are self-hosted (static/fonts, static/img).
- Honest copy (DESIGN P7): no invented logos, stats, testimonials. Sample data is from example-report.md.
- Motion: GSAP, transform/opacity only, honors prefers-reduced-motion, content readable without JS.
